Chemo Fever Card

Chemo ke mareez ke liye wallet card: ek side patient ke liye, doosri emergency staff ke liye (English), saath mein QR. Sab kuch sirf aap ke browser mein rehta hai.

Bukhar ki had aur hidayat apni oncology team se poochh kar likhein. Yeh app koi medical salah nahi deta, sirf aap ki di hui maloomat card par chhapta hai.
